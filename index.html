Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in your copywriting services?

▼

My services include comprehensive copywriting, strategic messaging development, and content planning. You'll receive polished, ready-to-use copy, along with strategic recommendations and revisions based on your feedback. I also provide clear explanations of the strategic choices behind the writing to help you understand the approach.

How long does a typical project take?

▼

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. A website copywriting project typically takes 2-4 weeks, while email campaigns or smaller projects can be completed in 1-2 weeks. I always provide a clear timeline upfront and keep you updated throughout the process to ensure we meet your deadlines.

Do you work with businesses in specific industries?

▼

I work with businesses across various industries, including technology, healthcare, finance, e-commerce, professional services, and more. My approach is research-driven, so I invest time in understanding your industry, audience, and competitive landscape to create copy that resonates authentically.

What's your revision policy?

▼

I include two rounds of revisions in every project to ensure the copy meets your expectations. This allows us to refine the messaging, adjust tone, and incorporate your feedback. Additional revisions can be arranged if needed, and I'm committed to working with you until you're satisfied with the final result.

How do you ensure the copy aligns with our brand voice?

▼

I start every project with a comprehensive discovery process that includes reviewing your existing brand materials, understanding your target audience, and discussing your brand values and communication style. This ensures the copy I create feels authentic to your brand while being strategically optimized for your goals.

Can you help with content strategy in addition to copywriting?

▼

Yes, I offer comprehensive content strategy services including editorial calendars, content frameworks, messaging guidelines, and strategic recommendations. Many clients find it valuable to combine copywriting with strategy to ensure consistency and alignment across all their content channels.
